Assembly

Digital joints first. LiPo on the back last — the order you described.

  1. Confirm the strip. USB flush at the bottom. XIAO, then SD on the same face, spare holes above the SD. BAT window already shows BAT+ / BAT−. Clip any header tails flush on that back face.
  2. SD to XIAO on the copper side. Short jumpers in the gap: 3V3→3V3, GND→GND, CLK→D9, CMD→D8, D0→D7, D3→D6. Leave SD D1 and SD D2 open. Leave XIAO 5V empty.
  3. 22 µF. Across the SD 3V3 and GND pins (they sit next to each other). Ceramic 16 V preferred. If electrolytic, stripe to GND.
  4. Button in the spare holes above the SD: NO → D1, COM → GND. Body sits on the FR4. Do not glue it into the lid.
  5. Meter, still no cell. 5V open. 3V3 not shorted to GND. D1 open to GND, short when pressed. SD VCC reads 3V3, not 5V.
  6. LiPo last. Cut the JST off the pack. Solder red to BAT+, black to BAT− through the window. Heat-shrink. Kapton the joints. Tape the pouch onto the back (PCM toward the window). Do not fold it.
  7. Case. Strip in the lid rails, USB in the bottom window, plunger in the Ø6.6 hole, mic under its hole. Snap shut. A dab of glue at the USB shoulders after a charge-port test.
  8. Power. USB first, no cell. Then cell. Then USB with cell (charges). Press the lid button.

Wear orientation

Conversation is in front of you, so the XIAO component face (mic) points away from your chest. The LiPo is the chest pad. IMU sleep will see walking motion; that is expected. Charge without opening the case: plug USB-C into the bottom window.

3D-printed case

Two-piece snap clamshell sized to your strip: XIAO + SD on the front, LiPo on the back. About 26 × 59 × 18 mm closed (with bail). USB-C on the bottom. Ø6.6 hole over the button.

  2. Seat the electronics. LiPo against the back floor (already taped to the strip). USB-C in the bottom window, button plunger in the Ø6.6 lid hole, mic under its hole. Do not pinch the pouch.
  3. Close and charge. Snap the lid. Plug USB-C with the necklace on or off. The BQ25101 on the XIAO still charges the 1S cell. Red LED should show through the small hole.
If the USB plug does not seat, the XIAO is not flush with the strip’s bottom edge — pull it to the USB end before the joints set, and reprint only after a dry-fit in the open lid.
