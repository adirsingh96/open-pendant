Orbit O case. The card stays inside the shells. Open the
two halves to take it out. There is no slot in the outer wall.

Printer
  0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers.
  PETG. PLA is only for a fit check.
  Front: skirt on the bed. No supports.
  Back: chest face on the bed, cavity up. The chain bail is at the
  top edge and its underside is flush with the chest face, so it
  sits on the bed. No support.
  Button hat: wide disc on the bed, head pointing up.

Chain
  One bail on the back, centered at the top. The hole is 5 mm and
  runs left to right. About 1.6 mm of plastic is around it. Use a
  chain or cord a little under 5 mm.
  The bail stops below the front skirt, so the lid still snaps on.

Snap
  The skirt is the spring. Each long side has two catches, 8 mm
  long, with a ramp so the lid slides on and a flat top that holds
  it. Pry at the notch in the middle of each side. The old teeth
  are gone.

Card
  The socket and the card sit in the cavity. Nothing is cut through
  the top wall. To change the card, unsnap the front shell.

Board
  The real board is the mirror of the KiCad file. USB-C down, the
  button is on the left. The case matches that.
  The USB-C metal face is the end of the case. The opening is only
  the shape of the connector, so a cable plugs straight in. The two
  corners on that end are round, the same as the top. Reprint
  the front and the back.
  The cell pocket is 1.5 mm deeper than the cell, so the red and
  black leads can lie on the cell and the board still sits down.
  The case is 1.5 mm thicker because of that.
  The board drops into the back shell. Stops at the USB end and the
  top leave 0.15 mm. The stops on the long sides leave 0.5 mm, so
  the board does not shear them off on the way in. The lid keeps
  the board down. Nothing is glued.

The front
  The logo is a tall O drawn as an orbit, centered on the lid. It is
  an inlay, not a relief: pockets 0.6 mm deep, filled flush, so the
  face is flat and nothing catches on clothes or a thumb. The ring
  is 3.5 mm wide. The O is the only art on the face.
  The pocket floor leaves 0.6 mm of lid under the art. The only holes
  through that wall are the ones that were already there: button,
  mic, LED.
  The button hat is the planet on the upper right of the ring. Its
  head is 5.2 mm across, sits in a 6.6 mm hole so a ring of black
  shell shows around it, and stands 1 mm proud of the face. That is
  the one bump kept: it is how a finger finds the button.

Colors, in Bambu Studio (P1S + AMS)
  The front is ONE print. The inlay is not a piece you print
  separately and stick in: it is the white part of the same model.
  In each of the top three layers the printer lays black around
  the O and white inside it, and the AMS swaps between them.

  1. Load character_front.3mf. Remove any older copy first.
  2. Set the project filaments to the spools in your AMS:
     filament 1 black, filament 2 white.
  3. Click the "Objects" tab at the top of the left panel, next
     to "Global". Expand the model. Two parts: Front_Shell and
     Orbit_Inlay. If you only see one part, right-click the model
     and choose Split, then To Parts.
  4. Front_Shell gets filament 1. Click Orbit_Inlay, right-click,
     Change Filament, filament 2. The O turns white on screen.
     Before this step you cannot see it: the inlay is flush and
     the same color as the shell.
  5. Select the model and click Arrange so it sits on the plate.
     Check from the top view: the flat face with the O points up,
     the open skirt points down. Do not flip it.
  6. The floating cantilever warning is the inside ceiling of
     the lid. It bridges the hollow shell and is hidden inside
     the closed case. No supports. Ignore the warning.
  7. Prime tower on. Slice, then check the Preview from the top:
     the white O appears in the last three layers.
  character_front.stl is the same model without part names.
  Button hat: all white, its own print. It is the planet, and it
  drops into the shell from inside. Nothing is glued.
  Back shell: all black, its own print.
  In Fusion the same split is appearances: black shells, white
  inlay body, white hat.
